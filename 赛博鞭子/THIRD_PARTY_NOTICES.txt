OpenWhip reference and audio attribution
--------------------------------------

Source: the user-supplied OpenWhip-main.zip.
Project name/version in package.json: openwhip 1.1.0.
Repository declared by the supplied package:
https://github.com/GitFrog1111/OpenWhip
License declared by the supplied package.json: MIT.

The rope simulation and interaction are based on the supplied project as a
reference and reimplemented for Windows .NET Framework / Win32.
The five original sound clips (A.mp3 to E.mp3) were converted to mono,
22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV with reduced amplitude for SoundPlayer playback.
No Electron, koffi, npm, or other original runtime dependencies are bundled.
The supplied source ZIP did not contain a separate LICENSE file.
